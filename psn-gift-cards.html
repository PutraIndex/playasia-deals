Top up your PlayStation Network wallet for less - digital codes delivered by email.

What is a PSN gift card?

A PlayStation Network gift card adds funds straight to your PSN wallet, so you can buy games, add-ons, subscriptions like PS Plus, and in-game content without linking a credit card to your console.

Why buy PSN cards at a discount?

  • Discounted wallet codes effectively mean cheaper games - the savings stack on top of any PSN sale
  • No credit card needed on your console
  • Great for gifting to PlayStation players
  • Region options let you access other storefronts' prices

How it works

Pick your card value and region, check out, and the digital code arrives by email - usually within minutes. Redeem it on your console or through the PlayStation Store website and the funds land in your wallet instantly.

Tips before you buy

Match the card region to your PSN account region - a US card only funds a US account. If a card looks too cheap compared to face value, check that the region suits you before buying.
