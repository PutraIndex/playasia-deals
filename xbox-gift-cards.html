What is an Xbox gift card?

An Xbox gift card adds funds to your Microsoft account balance, spendable on console and PC games, add-ons, and Game Pass subscriptions.

Why buy Xbox cards at a discount

How it works

Pick the value, check out, and the digital code arrives by email. Redeem at microsoft.com/redeem or on your console and the funds land in your account instantly.
