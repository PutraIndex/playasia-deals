Why import from Japan?

Japan gets exclusive special editions, earlier release dates for many titles, and unique games that never leave the region. Importing means access to all of it - often at prices competitive with local releases once you factor in sales.

What you can find

  • Japan-exclusive special and collector's editions

  • Retro and out-of-print titles long gone from Western stores
  • Anime and visual novel games with limited print runs

Language considerations

Most modern Nintendo Switch imports include English or are fully playable without Japanese knowledge. PlayStation discs are region-free but game language depends on the title - check the product page's language notes before buying.
